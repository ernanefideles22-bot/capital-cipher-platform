# Capital Cipher — Governed Strategy Research Lab (Phase D0)

Status: **research foundation, not wired to execution**. No LIVE, Gate Testnet, PAPER
routing, risk limits, historical data, or scheduler changes in this PR.

## Principle

300 agents do not equal 300 independent pieces of evidence. Agents may *propose*
new strategies, but cannot write arbitrary trading code into the running
system, promote themselves, change stop/risk controls, or submit orders.

A strategy is a versioned falsifiable hypothesis, not an indicator vote.
A research hypothesis declares: author, market/timeframe, side, conditions,
invalidation, risk/target parameters and thesis. Deterministic rules are
whitelisted. Any new feature/expression requires code review and tests.

## Research families to investigate (none declared profitable by default)

1. Trend-following pullback: higher-timeframe structure + EMA + ATR stop.
2. Confirmed breakout: prior range, relative volume, fake-out conditions.
3. Time-series momentum conditional on regime and volatility.
4. Range mean reversion with strict trend exclusion.
5. Market structure + candle rejection as *confirmatory*, not stand-alone signals.
6. Novel combinations generated by agents from bounded, reviewed predicates.

Fibonacci/RSI/MACD are potential features; existence of a pattern or high
directional accuracy is NOT proof of net trading alpha.

## Proposed workflow

```text
Trusted closed OHLCV feed
       -> point-in-time market snapshot
       -> PRIMARY and SHADOW agent research proposals
       -> immutable hypothesis + fingerprint
       -> offline parameter search on training ONLY
       -> purged temporal CV / walk-forward OOS + a sealed final holdout
       -> costs, liquidity, funding, leverage and venue-netting stress tests
       -> multiple-testing corrected statistics and benchmark comparison
       -> independent result attestation
       -> manual review -> separate SHADOW campaign
       -> human-authorized PAPER promotion through existing OMS/risk gate
```

## Technical boundaries in this incremental PR

- `market_intelligence.analyze_closed_candles` uses >=60 strictly ordered
  fully closed OHLCV bars, rejects future timestamps and emits context only.
  EMA/ATR/volume/pattern thresholds are **descriptive heuristics** whose
  economic validity is untested.
- `research_lab.StrategyHypothesis` is an immutable, fingerprinted proposal
  with bounded features and numerical parameters. A novel *combination* can be
  proposed; an entirely new executable operator requires review.
- `research_lab.review_hypothesis` returns either `RESEARCH_ONLY` or
  `ELIGIBLE_FOR_HUMAN_REVIEW`. **Both have execution_authorized=False.**
  Thresholds (100 OOS trades, >=3 folds, 12 bps per side stressed cost,
  15% max drawdown and p<0.05 corrected) are provisional governance defaults,
  not validated optimal parameters.
- `OutOfSampleEvidence` is a data contract only; its booleans/digest **do
  not cryptographically establish independent evaluation**. Before wiring
  promotion or UI, implement server-side attestation under a separate
  privileged evaluator that agents cannot impersonate, with immutable run IDs,
  dataset hashes, code commit, parameters and temporal folds.
- No integration with OMS, PAPER order APIs, execution adapters or timers.
- Existing recorded PAPER losses and 3 open positions are not changed.

## Necessary follow-on stages before any trading activation

- D1: Repository-backed research audit log and actual feature-extraction
  dataset snapshots with input timestamp validation and source hashes.
- D2: Independent, sequential no-lookahead replay; purged walk-forward with
  embargo, correction for multiple searches, realistic costs and slippage,
  stress under liquidity/market regimes; no free use of future labels.
- D3: Counterfactual agent attribution from decision ID through risk check
  to signed PAPER closure; compare feature ablations against the baseline.
- D4: Paper simulation must match Gate one-way netting or use explicitly
  isolated independent books; existing same-symbol opposing ETH positions are
  a fidelity issue.
- D5: Only passing hypotheses enter limited SHADOW evaluation. Separate human
  approval plus the existing central risk/OMS is required for PAPER. Testnet
  and LIVE need new, explicit owner authorization and additional evidence.
- D6: Drift detection and retirement: demote strategies as regimes change.
  No silent online weight/parameter update in execution.

## Market research notes (not guarantees)

Recent evidence is mixed and regime-dependent. Time-series momentum may retain
more robust evidence than cross-sectional crypto momentum once real-world
frictions and liquidations are considered. A 2026 survivor-bias study showed
that some apparent momentum payoffs depend on transiently tradable coins.
Another 2026 ML walk-forward study found gross profitable signals collapsing
after 10 bps costs, with selected cost-aware filters faring better.

- https://academic.oup.com/raps/advance-article-abstract/doi/10.1093/rapstu/raag011/8914819
- https://www.sciencedirect.com/science/article/pii/S1544612326001339
- https://papers.ssrn.com/sol3/papers.cfm?abstract_id=6795938

Never infer today's profitability of Capital Cipher from external publications.
Its own OOS data and execution constraints are the promotion criteria.
